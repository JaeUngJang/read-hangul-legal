Support for Read Hangul in 30 Minutes

Found a problem, heard a pronunciation that seems off, or have an idea? Email jjw3435@gmail.com.

For a bug report, include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version, and what happened. A screenshot or the letter you were practicing can help. Please do not include sensitive information.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Lessons, picture mnemonics, quizzes, and included pronunciation clips work offline. The block builder needs an installed Korean system voice for speech. Opening external links, posting to social apps, and using App Store services may require an internet connection.

Why is the block builder silent?

Check your device volume and audio output. The builder uses the iPhone's Korean system voice. If no Korean voice is installed, the app shows that speech is unavailable. You can manage system voices in iPhone Accessibility settings.

Where is my progress saved?

Progress and earned badges are saved on your device. There is no account or developer-run cloud sync. Deleting the app removes its local data. Apple backups may preserve data, depending on your settings.

Can I keep learning after earning a badge?

Yes. You can revisit your badge and choose to learn again. You can also share your achievement using the iOS share sheet.

Is this a language proficiency certificate?

The badge celebrates practice in the app. It is not an accredited qualification or a certification of Korean fluency.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles App Store payments and refund requests. Visit Apple's Report a Problem page to request help with a purchase.
