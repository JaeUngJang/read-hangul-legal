Privacy Policy

Read Hangul in 30 Minutes
Last updated: October 5, 2026

This policy describes how unglazed ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in the Read Hangul in 30 Minutes iOS app, on these support pages, and when you contact us.

The app does not collect personal data or send your learning activity to us. It has no account, advertising, analytics SDK, or tracking. Learning works on your device.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your place in the lessons, letters you have practiced, quiz results, earned badges and their dates, and whether a review request has been attempted. If you add a name to your badge, that optional name is also saved locally. These records let you continue learning and revisit your achievements.

We do not receive these records. The app does not operate an account or a developer-run cloud sync service. Apple device backups may include app data, depending on your settings.

2. Audio and device access

Pronunciation clips are included with the app. The block builder uses an installed Korean system voice. The app does not record your voice, request microphone access, or send your input to an AI service. Some included clips were created with AI during production; playing them does not contact that service.

3. Sharing is your choice

When you choose to share an achievement, the app creates an image on your device and opens the iOS share sheet. The image and accompanying message may include your achievement, a date, and any optional name you have added. Only the destination you choose receives what you share. That destination's privacy practices apply. Review the preview before sharing.

4. Purchases, reviews, and Apple services

Apple handles App Store purchases, payments, and reviews. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide developers with store reports or diagnostics under its own services and your settings. We do not add an in-app analytics or crash-reporting service. See Apple's Privacy Policy for information about its services, including backups.

5. If you contact us

If you email jjw3435@gmail.com, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include. We use this information to answer your request and resolve the issue, and keep it only as long as needed for those purposes or applicable obligations. Email providers process the message as part of delivering and storing email. Please avoid sending sensitive information.

6. These web pages

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We have not added cookies, analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address when serving the pages under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This is separate from using the app offline.

7. Your choices and deletion

You can leave the badge name blank or remove it in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app may preserve it. You can manage backups separately in your Apple settings. Copies you share are controlled by you and the destination where you shared them.

We cannot retrieve or delete learning records from your device remotely. To request access, correction, or deletion of information you have sent us by email, or to ask about any privacy rights available in your region, contact us below.

8. Children and policy updates

The app does not collect personal data from learners, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information in a support message, please contact us so we can address it.

We may update this policy if the app or our practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version.
